### Car — third party, key terms

From the Clal renewal schedule dated 2026-02-06. Cover runs 2026-03-01 →
2027-02-28 on the Honda Jazz.

- **Not comprehensive.** Chapter A (own damage) was excluded at your request —
  damage to your own car isn't covered, only damage you cause to others.
- Third-party property limit: ₪1,000,000
- Excess on a third-party property claim: ₪1,239
- Criminal legal defence: up to ₪15,000, excess ₪350
- **Drivers under 24, or with under 48 months' experience in this vehicle type,
  are not covered.** Worth knowing before lending the car.

## Annual review

Once a year, top to bottom. Most of the money saved on insurance is saved here.

- [ ] List every active policy and what it actually costs per year
- [ ] Check for duplicate cover — the same risk insured twice
- [ ] Health: check what the public plan / HMO already covers before paying extra
- [ ] Home: is the contents valuation still realistic? Update after big purchases
- [ ] Home: confirm water damage and theft are actually included
- [ ] Car: claim the no-claims discount you've earned
- [ ] Travel: check credit card cover before buying a separate policy
- [ ] Get comparison quotes for the two most expensive policies
- [ ] Cancel anything you're paying for and can't justify
