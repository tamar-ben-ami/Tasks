# Before you go

The admin that blocks the trip. Roughly in the order it needs doing.

⚠️ Entry rules, fees and levies change. Treat everything here as "check this",
not as settled fact — confirm on the official sites
(immigration.govt.nz, nzta.govt.nz) before relying on any of it.

## Entry

| What | Needed by | Done | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Passports valid well past the return date | | | Many carriers want 6 months. See [../../documents.md](../../documents.md) |
| NZeTA | | | Electronic travel authority, applied for online or in the app |
| IVL (visitor levy) | | | Usually paid together with the NZeTA |

- [ ] Check passport expiry dates first — everything else depends on them
- [ ] Confirm Israeli passports are still visa-waiver eligible, and for how long
      a stay
- [ ] Apply for the NZeTA — it is not instant, so don't leave it to the last week
- [ ] Keep the NZeTA confirmation somewhere reachable offline

## Driving

- [ ] Confirm what NZ accepts alongside an Israeli licence — an International
      Driving Permit, or an official English translation
- [ ] Get the IDP or translation sorted before leaving; it can't be done there
- [ ] Check the minimum age and any young-driver surcharge with the hire company
- [ ] Read up on one-lane bridges and passing lanes before the first drive

## Insurance

- [ ] Travel insurance, with cover for hiking and any adventure activities —
      standard policies often exclude exactly what people go to NZ to do
- [ ] Check what the credit card already covers before buying separately
- [ ] Check whether the car hire excess is worth buying down
- [ ] Record the policy in [../../insurance.md](../../insurance.md)

Note: NZ's ACC scheme covers accidental injury for visitors, but it is not
health insurance and does not cover illness or repatriation. Don't travel on it.

## Health

- [ ] Check whether any vaccinations are recommended
- [ ] Enough of any regular medication for the whole trip, in original packaging
      with the prescription — see [../../health.md](../../health.md)
- [ ] Basic first aid kit, plus sunscreen: the UV is genuinely harsher than the
      temperature suggests

## Money

- [ ] Tell the bank and card issuers the travel dates
- [ ] Check foreign transaction fees, and take a backup card kept separately
- [ ] Card works almost everywhere; a small amount of cash for remote areas

## Biosecurity — read this one

New Zealand's border controls are strict and actively enforced. An undeclared
apple can mean an instant fine.

- [ ] Declare all food, plant and animal products. Declaring is free; not
      declaring is not
- [ ] Clean hiking boots thoroughly — soil is a problem, and boots get inspected
- [ ] Same for tent pegs, walking poles and any camping gear that has touched dirt
- [ ] When unsure, declare it. There is no penalty for declaring

## Home while away

- [ ] Someone covering post, plants, and the flat
- [ ] Heating/water set sensibly for the season — see [../../home.md](../../home.md)
- [ ] Bills on standing order won't bounce while away
