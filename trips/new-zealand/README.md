## Decide first

These three shape everything else, so settle them before booking anything.

- [ ] **When.** Seasons are flipped — NZ summer is December to February, which is
      also peak season and peak prices. March–April and October–November are
      quieter, cheaper, and still good for walking. June–August is winter.
- [ ] **How long.** Both islands in under two weeks means most of it is spent
      travelling. One island done properly beats two done at a sprint.
- [ ] **North, South, or both.** South Island for mountains, glaciers, fjords and
      the big walks; North for coast, volcanoes, Māori culture and the cities.

## Worth knowing early

- **There are no direct flights from Tel Aviv.** Expect 25–30 hours with one or
  two stops, and a full day lost each way. Build recovery days into the plan.
- **NZ is 10–11 hours ahead of Israel**, depending on daylight saving in both.
- **Driving is on the left**, and distances take longer than the map suggests —
  roads are narrow and winding. A 300 km day is a real day.
- **The Great Walks book out months ahead**, often the day bookings open. If any
  of those are wanted, that deadline drives the whole trip.
- **Biosecurity is strict and enforced.** See [before-you-go.md](before-you-go.md).
