# 🔁 Recurring

The things that come back around. This is the file to check when you have a
free hour and a vague feeling you're forgetting something.

Nothing here is ever "done" — reset the boxes when a cycle comes around.

## Weekly

- [ ] Plan groceries → [groceries.md](groceries.md)
- [ ] Sort the inbox → [inbox.md](inbox.md)
- [ ] Bills and mail — anything that arrived needing action

## Monthly

- [ ] Review spending against the budget → [money.md](money.md)
- [ ] Scan card statements for charges you don't recognize
- [ ] Check standing orders and subscriptions — cancel what you stopped using

## Quarterly

- [ ] Car: tire pressure, oil, wipers → [car.md](car.md)
- [ ] Back up photos and important documents
- [ ] Deep-clean / maintenance round at home → [home.md](home.md)
